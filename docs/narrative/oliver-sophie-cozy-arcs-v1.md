# Bellwick — two people who make the shop feel like home

**Status:** authored character + playable dialogue proposals, not implemented. **Language:** Ukrainian. **Placement:** Oliver Day 2–4; Sophie Day 1–5. **Purpose:** genuine lightness, humor and companionship, without secret trauma or detective clues.

## Oliver — café worker, 33

**Core:** warm, mildly disorganized, a lover of silly science fiction and board games. Likes his ordinary life. Not a hidden villain, not a therapist, not a future tragedy. Can be competitive about trivial things and forget the punchline of his own joke.

**Visual:** sleeves rolled up, slightly crooked apron after shift, carries a thermos or paper bag. Never always standing at the same shelf.

**Recurring behavior:** brings the wrong pastry and admits it; exchanges recommendations with the player; sometimes leaves without buying anything. May sit with Frank, who prefers his warm coat to his attention.

## Sophie — florist, 26

**Core:** likes Bellwick and does not dream of leaving. Observant, playful, caring, sometimes stubborn about décor and a catastrophically bad loser at games. Her life is not a disguised tragedy. Not a constant ray of sunshine: she gets tired and has preferences.

**Visual:** practical boots, cardigan with bits of leaf, small floral scissors safely sheathed, carries seasonal bunches. She should sometimes visit for her own reading, not only to deliver decorations.

**Recurring behavior:** changes flowers based on season; gives plants names; argues that books and plants have equally demanding owners; likes Frank but respects when he walks away.

### Sophie scene 1 — «Квіти, які ніхто не замовляв» (Day 1 or 2, 5–7 min)

*Софі приносить маленький букет польових квітів.*

**Софі:** «Це не замовлення. Я просто подумала, що біля каси занадто серйозно».
**Гравець:** «У нас книжковий магазин».
**Софі:** «Саме тому. Книжки вже стараються виглядати важливими».

Choose:
- «Де їх поставимо?» → DECOR

**Софі:** «Не біля Френка. Він минулого разу вирішив, що ваза — це особистий ворог».
**Гравець:** «Може, на підвіконня?»
**Софі:** «Там сонце. І кіт. Обирай, кому довіряєш більше».
Choices:
- «На високу полицю». → **Софі:** «Нарешті рішення дорослої людини».
- «На стіл біля входу». → **Софі:** «Добре. Тільки залиш місце для ліктів».
- «Поки залишимо в тебе». → **Софі:** «Я принесу меншу вазу. Без образ».

HABIT:

**Гравець:** «Чому сьогодні подарунок?»
**Софі:** «Бо мені подобається, коли в цьому магазині пахне не лише папером. Хоча папір теж чудово пахне».

**Софі:** «Зрозуміло. Я заберу. Якщо передумаєш — зайди. У мене є вази, які не займають половину міста».

## Pacing and cast balance

- Oliver and Sophie do not join the main mystery as suspects, clue carriers, or mandatory witnesses.
- Oliver's visits can be skipped entirely; Sophie may appear only as décor support if the player avoids conversations.
- Their positive moments must not always be interrupted by urgent plot beats.
- Preserve variety: Oliver uses playful exaggeration and self-deprecation; Sophie is brisk, opinionated and socially confident.
- Avoid making Sophie a romantic reward or Oliver a comic-relief machine. They can disagree with the player, then talk about something else.
- No hidden “everyone is secretly broken” reversal in a later chapter.
- Treat age, job, visual details and backstory as proposed canon until cross-checked with current character bible.
- All books and minigames here are narrative placeholders; do not assume assets or catalog entries exist.
- A cat-safe arrangement is required for floral props; don't imply unknown plants are safe for cats. Use fictional or vetted non-toxic décor in implementation.

## Integration acceptance

1. Each scene offers a short exit with no relationship penalty.
2. No evidence, rumor or mystery state changes.
3. Optional choices affect only local banter or clearly scoped cosmetic detail.
4. Repeated visits don't replay the same introduction without player choice.
5. Mobile UI: max three choices on screen at a time (paginate if needed), no canvas resizing.
6. All story text Ukrainian; proper names follow project's English-origin naming convention.
7. Do not treat these docs as shipped content until runtime integration and QA are complete.
