# LAST CHAPTER — Character Production Bible V2

**Status:** editorial production specification; not all beats are implemented. **Language:** Ukrainian for player-facing material. **Scope:** eight established human characters, player protagonist, and Frank. Do not silently introduce proposed Oksana, Danylo or Sofia into playable continuity. Eleanor is established canon.

## Dramatic contract

**Central question:** «Чи маємо ми право розповідати чужу історію, навіть коли віримо, що допомагаємо?» The player is a bookseller, not a detective with a right to everyone's secrets. Every character must have a meaningful life beyond the mystery and at least one moment in which they disagree with the player without becoming an antagonist.

**Five-day rhythm:** D1 acquaintance and surface need; D2 contradiction; D3 personal cost; D4 autonomous choice; D5 a modest, earned change. Not every character must appear every day. A short conversation must be complete in itself; optional deep conversations should reward curiosity with understanding, not exclusive “good” outcomes.

**Evidence discipline:** observed / reported / inferred / unknown. Do not conflate who wrote the letter, who saw it, who knew about it, and who was entitled to read it.

## 01 — Беатріс Міллер · The storyteller who fears being forgotten

**Canon:** Day 1 visit; town rumors; Day 5 callback. Older regular customer, collector of detective novels, jam jars, and stories.

- **Outer desire:** buy a clever detective novel and keep the neighborhood connected.
- **Hidden need:** to matter to people without being the person who knows everybody's secrets.
- **Wound:** growing older made her feel less consulted; she fills silence with narratives. This is a proposed motivation, not yet revealed in dialogue.
- **Virtue / shadow:** attentive to small details / turns details into certainty too quickly.
- **Voice:** brisk, affectionate, mischievous, sharp metaphors, never a generic exposition dispenser.
- **Contradiction:** champions evidence in fiction but sometimes accepts rumors in real life.
- **Relationships:** Eleanor shares decades of shop memories; Thomas is affected by her speculation; Nora pushes her to check sources.
- **D1:** witness versus inference, the ambiguous sighting; optional conversation about memory.
- **D2:** proposed quiet moment: realizes a rumor has consequences; do not make her confess to an event she did not cause.
- **D3:** hears another account, can ask rather than assume.
- **D4:** proposed choice: correct a rumor privately rather than perform a public apology.
- **D5:** if `miller_observation_distinguished`, acknowledges uncertainty without self-erasure.
- **Deep topics:** why mysteries feel safe; loneliness in a small town; memory as social glue; whether silence is kindness; how to correct yourself in public.
- **Distinct outcomes:** curiosity without proof; rumor challenged; apology offered but not necessarily accepted.
- **Sample:** «У книжках я люблю, коли все пояснюють. У житті, мабуть, надто часто пояснюю сама».
- **Avoid:** reducing an older woman to comic gossip or treating age as cognitive incompetence.

## 02 — Мара · The person who refuses to become someone else's lesson

**Canon:** baker, Leo's partner, Day 1 book gift, Day 4 autonomy and boundaries, Day 5 callback.

- **Outer desire:** choose a meaningful gift for Leo and keep her work and relationship afloat.
- **Hidden need:** to want something for herself without feeling selfish.
- **Wound:** accustomed to being reliable and available; specific family backstory remains intentionally open.
- **Virtue / shadow:** generous and perceptive / may overfunction to avoid difficult conversations.
- **Voice:** warm, quick humor, concrete details of bread, shifts, and small rituals; not a therapist.
- **Contradiction:** admires dramatic romantic devotion in books while learning that devotion without boundaries can exhaust real people.
- **Relationships:** Leo is her current partner, not an obstacle to an Iris–Leo reunion. Iris has an independent history; Mara does not exist to judge her.
- **D1:** gift selection; optional debate on redemption, change and forgiveness.
- **D2–3:** proposed indirect follow-through via book, bakery, or Leo's recollection; no forced encounter.
- **D4:** declines an extra shift; imagines a morning reading table; names the right to choose.
- **D5:** callback if `mara_day_four_reflected`; her future is hers, irrespective of letter outcome.
- **Deep topics:** Tamlin-versus-Rhysand-style discussions as interpretive debate, never declaring fictional preferences evidence of real-life character; boundaries versus punishment; whether a gift can be a demand; romance and safety; ambition without guilt.
- **Distinct outcomes:** `mara_discussed_forgiveness`, `mara_day_four_reflected`, `mara_future_supported`; none should turn her into a reward for “correct” answers.
- **Sample:** «Я хочу бути поруч із людьми. Але іноді хочу просто сісти, допити каву й нікому нічого не винна».
- **Avoid:** love triangle as spectacle; equating forgiveness with reconciliation.

## 03 — Лео Марш · The man who mistakes planning for certainty

**Canon:** train schedules, book from Mara, old reservation, Iris's undelivered letter, Day 4 decision, Day 5 callback.

- **Outer desire:** understand what happened to an old reservation without losing the life he has now.
- **Hidden need:** admit uncertainty and speak directly rather than hide behind a timetable.
- **Wound:** a missed encounter seven years ago; no claim that he knowingly rejected Iris.
- **Virtue / shadow:** dependable, considerate / postpones difficult talks while calling it prudence.
- **Voice:** precise, observant, dry humor; becomes less syntactically controlled under pressure.
- **Contradiction:** trusts schedules but knows journeys are made by people.
- **Relationships:** Mara is his present; Iris is a person from his past, not a mystery prize; Anna knows his more generous side.
- **D1:** appears through Mara's account and the reservation.
- **D2:** his own testimony complicates assumptions.
- **D3:** proposed private reflection on the gap between intention and action.
- **D4:** may consent to a conversation, or may need time.
- **D5:** `leo_shared_future_plan` unlocks a callback about choosing a journey without certainty.
- **Deep topics:** regret without self-punishment; whether an unsent message counts as a choice; promises and contingency; being kind versus being clear.
- **Distinct outcomes:** conversation offered, deferred, or declined; no automatic romance restoration.
- **Sample:** «У розкладі є час прибуття. А от коли людина буде готова говорити — такого стовпчика немає».
- **Avoid:** presenting Leo as owing Iris a relationship or Mara an explanation of Iris's private letter.

## 04 — Айріс Белл · The artist who wants ownership of her own story

**Canon:** author of sealed letter, artist of windows, Day 2 and Day 4 visits, Day 5 exhibition possibility.

- **Outer desire:** reclaim a connection to the shop without being reduced to the seven-year-old letter.
- **Hidden need:** let the past matter without allowing it to decide her future.
- **Wound:** the letter did not reach its intended destination; its consequences belong to her, not the player.
- **Virtue / shadow:** observant, imaginative / rehearses explanations rather than risking vulnerability.
- **Voice:** visual language, windows and light; measured when guarded, unexpectedly direct when respected.
- **Contradiction:** draws interiors from outside because she wants closeness and distance at once.
- **Relationships:** Leo is one chapter, Mara is a full person in the present, Eleanor remembers Iris drawing by the shop window.
- **D1:** letter appears without revealing her words.
- **D2:** she may share as much or little as she wants.
- **D3:** proposed art context; her work must have a life outside romance.
- **D4:** chooses how to handle the letter; can propose an exhibition.
- **D5:** exhibition, private talk, postponement, or boundary; admitted privacy breach never guarantees forgiveness.
- **Deep topics:** identity after leaving home; art as witnessing versus trespassing; nostalgia; what makes a place safe; whether closure requires an answer.
- **Distinct outcomes:** `iris_letter_choice_respected`, `iris_offered_exhibition`, `iris_letter_breach_admitted`, `final_accepted_consequences`.
- **Sample:** «Я намалювала світло у вікні. Це не означає, що я маю право знати, хто там живе».
- **Avoid:** public reading of the private letter, romantic “reunion” as default success, compulsory absolution.

## 05 — Анна Гейл · The teacher who has forgotten how to ask

**Canon:** teacher, reading club, Thomas's wife, Day 2 visit and Day 5 gathering.

- **Outer desire:** choose a book that gets her students talking honestly.
- **Hidden need:** accept that care is reciprocal and she does not need to anticipate every question.
- **Wound:** years of organizing other people's lives; do not invent an affair or marital crisis from Miller's rumor.
- **Virtue / shadow:** patient, intellectually curious / may turn every emotion into a lesson plan.
- **Voice:** open questions, light self-deprecation, occasional tired directness.
- **Contradiction:** encourages students to be imperfect while holding herself to impossible standards.
- **Relationships:** Thomas is a separate person, not evidence in a rumor; Leo shares a quieter friendship; Nora challenges her interpretations.
- **D1:** rumor concerns her family; never treated as fact.
- **D2:** selects a book or leaves with questions.
- **D3:** book-club discussion: who gets to define a character's motives?
- **D4:** proposed beat: asks someone else to lead a task.
- **D5:** book-club outcome depends on the kind of discussion fostered, not sales volume.
- **Deep topics:** the pressure to be a good adult; reading with children; how to disagree without humiliation; teaching uncertainty; asking for help.
- **Distinct outcomes:** `anna_chose_mystery` and other established book-choice flags; future content should distinguish curiosity from compliance.
- **Sample:** «Я кажу дітям, що не знати — нормально. А сама досі соромлюся поставити просте запитання».
- **Avoid:** using her solely to validate Thomas or as a passive victim of gossip.

## 06 — Томас Гейл · The fixer who cannot repair time

**Canon:** electrician, Anna's husband, Day 3 visit, subject of town speculation.

- **Outer desire:** sort out a practical matter tied to the shop and old records.
- **Hidden need:** recognize that fixing a thing does not settle how people felt about its loss.
- **Wound:** an unresolved past obligation; specifics must remain anchored to existing scene evidence.
- **Virtue / shadow:** practical, loyal / substitutes useful labor for vulnerable speech.
- **Voice:** plain, economical, specific about tools and places; emotional precision arrives late.
- **Contradiction:** can identify a fault in wiring but cannot always name a problem between people.
- **Relationships:** Anna deserves truth, not a plot twist; Miller's interpretation is not a verdict; Nora may offer an alternative chronology.
- **D1:** named in gossip, absent and unable to defend himself.
- **D2:** proposed offstage consequence: player can choose not to spread the story.
- **D3:** direct testimony complicates the rumor; player must distinguish what he knows from what he guesses.
- **D4:** proposed small repair offered without expecting gratitude.
- **D5:** can be present without a public confession.
- **Deep topics:** apology versus restitution; masculinity and usefulness; keeping a promise you can no longer fulfill; being misunderstood.
- **Distinct outcomes:** protect privacy, question evidence, or perpetuate a rumor; no sensational affair reveal.
- **Sample:** «Дріт можна замінити. Зі словами складніше: вони іноді лишаються там, де їх уже ніхто не хотів».
- **Avoid:** making his marriage a puzzle for the player to solve.

## 07 — Нора · The archivist who wants every question answered

**Canon:** Day 3 visit and book-club arc; exact profession and age should be confirmed against existing dialogue before being fixed as canon.

- **Outer desire:** reconcile conflicting details in records and stories.
- **Hidden need:** learn that a correct question does not confer a right to the answer.
- **Wound:** uncertainty once cost her something important; the specific event is proposed, not established.
- **Virtue / shadow:** rigorous and curious / risks treating people as sources.
- **Voice:** precise questions, thoughtful pauses, humor through unexpected analogies.
- **Contradiction:** respects the integrity of documents but may overlook the privacy of their authors.
- **Relationships:** Miller offers stories, Eleanor offers context, Thomas knows a different piece of the timeline.
- **D1–2:** optional foreshadowing through records, not mandatory appearances.
- **D3:** central discussion on evidence, provenance and the ethics of knowing.
- **D4:** proposed choice: leave a sealed question sealed.
- **D5:** no grand reveal required; a carefully framed uncertainty can be her growth.
- **Deep topics:** why ambiguity feels unbearable; archival gaps; curiosity versus entitlement; how stories survive imperfect records.
- **Distinct outcomes:** asks permission, notes uncertainty, or pushes too hard; preserve respectful disagreement.
- **Sample:** «Порожнє місце в архіві — це теж факт. Але не обов’язково доказ того, що хтось щось приховав».
- **Avoid:** omniscient detective exposition.

## 08 — Елеонора · The former keeper of the shop

**Canon:** old keys, flood, reservation ledger, Day 3 visit, Day 5 closure; established, not a proposed extra.

- **Outer desire:** know the bookshop will survive in new hands.
- **Hidden need:** stop confusing responsibility for the place with control over everyone in it.
- **Wound:** the flood and lost continuity; exact responsibility for an undelivered letter must remain evidentially uncertain.
- **Virtue / shadow:** generous keeper of communal memory / reluctant to relinquish authority.
- **Voice:** dry wit, tactile details of shelves, dust, tea, old paper.
- **Contradiction:** wants the shop to change and instinctively corrects the position of every shelf.
- **Relationships:** Miller remembers a different version of the shop; Iris drew by its window; the player inherits its future, not Eleanor's decisions.
- **D1:** appears in others' memories and handwritten notes.
- **D2:** proposed consequence of the player's questions, no artificial new visit.
- **D3:** recounts the flood, repairs, and missing reservation with appropriate uncertainty.
- **D4:** proposed handover moment: lets the player make a choice she would not make.
- **D5:** closing the old register is a handover, not a death metaphor.
- **Deep topics:** ownership versus stewardship; guilt about imperfect care; how places hold memory; whether keeping everything is the same as protecting it.
- **Distinct outcomes:** `eleanor_shared_shop_memory`, `ask_eleanor_next_day`; memory is not proof of culpability.
- **Sample:** «Я зберегла майже всі чеки. Шкода, що не можна так само акуратно складати дні».
- **Avoid:** making her a secret villain or conveniently omniscient keeper of all answers.

## 09 — Гравець / новий книгар · The listener with a choice

**Canon:** player-controlled shopkeeper; deliberately avoid imposing a fixed biography or gendered identity in player-facing Ukrainian.

- **Outer desire:** keep the shop functioning and serve visitors.
- **Internal question:** when is helping an invitation, and when is it intrusion?
- **Agency:** may listen briefly, explore deeply, ask for evidence, make mistakes, acknowledge harm, or leave questions unresolved.
- **D1:** learns the difference between selling a book and interpreting a person.
- **D2:** learns that different testimonies need not imply deceit.
- **D3:** learns the limits of investigation.
- **D4:** accepts that other people choose outcomes.
- **D5:** hosts a space rather than orchestrating everyone's lives.
- **Design:** never diagnose the player, moralize through hidden scores, or lock the respectful ending behind exhaustive dialogue.
- **Sample narration:** «Ти не знаєш, чим закінчиться ця розмова. Але можеш вирішити, як її почати».

## 10 — Френк · The gray Devon Rex who makes the shop feel inhabited

**Canon:** small gray Devon Rex; shop companion. No supernatural insight or human speech.

- **Outer desire:** warmth, safe places, food, and attention on his terms.
- **Dramatic role:** relief after heavy conversations; a reminder that care can be quiet and nonverbal.
- **Behavior:** climbs onto the returns tray, curls near the warm lamp, bats at a loose card, chooses whom to approach.
- **D1:** first interaction establishes consent: pet him, offer a hand, or leave him asleep.
- **D2:** optional observation after an emotionally difficult scene.
- **D3:** investigates a shelf without “solving” the mystery.
- **D4:** quiet pause with Iris or Mara; never a magical emotional shortcut.
- **D5:** sleeps through the reading, then approaches when the crowd thins.
- **Design:** accessible optional interactions, no penalty for skipping, no forced affection.
- **Sample:** «Френк обнюхав простягнуту долоню, подумав і пішов до батареї. Схоже, рішення було обґрунтованим».

## Relationship map and unresolved tensions

- **Mara ↔ Leo:** current partnership, everyday intimacy, room for direct talk; not collateral damage of a “better” romance.
- **Iris ↔ Leo:** old letter, incomplete context, independent right to talk or not talk.
- **Iris ↔ Mara:** no compulsory rivalry; each has interiority beyond Leo.
- **Anna ↔ Thomas:** shared life, never an investigation target.
- **Miller ↔ Thomas / Anna:** rumors demonstrate how interpretation can injure absent people.
- **Eleanor ↔ Miller:** different memories of the same place can coexist.
- **Eleanor ↔ Iris:** shop as refuge, art as memory.
- **Nora ↔ everyone:** good questions can be generous or invasive.
- **Player ↔ everyone:** hospitality is not ownership.
- **Frank ↔ shop:** recurring sensory motif, not a clue delivery machine.

## Five-day delivery gates

1. **Continuity pass:** every established character has consistent history, relationships, age/profession where confirmed, and no invented factual guilt.
2. **Dialogue pass:** one satisfying short route and one substantial optional route per established human character. Optional route has at least two meaningful perspectives, not padded exposition.
3. **Choice pass:** consequences surface later as dialogue or staging; never punish skipping optional conversations.
4. **State pass:** every flag used by a later scene is actually granted somewhere, and can survive save/load.
5. **Editorial pass:** native Ukrainian voice, differentiated speech, no manipulative therapist-speak, no privacy breach framed as romantic heroism.
6. **QA pass:** typecheck, unit tests, five-day browser playthrough and screenshots. None are claimed complete by this document.
