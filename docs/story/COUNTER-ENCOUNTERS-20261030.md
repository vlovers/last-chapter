# Counter encounters · final reading evening (2026-10-30)

Three optional, ordinary bookshop conversations were added to the final reading in `src/content/counter-encounters.ts`. This is everyday-life content, **not evidence** for the manuscript investigation.

## Character and choice contract

- **Ruth:** a tired shift worker choosing a book for her mother. The player can suggest asking her mother, checking large-print stock, or making a shortlist. No stock or sale is promised without confirmation.
- **Sam:** a bakery worker looking for a book for himself. His choices cover beginner dough, a funny memoir, or asking Mara for teaching time when she is available. Mara's consent is never assumed.
- **Mr Reed:** a secondhand book with a private note. The player can offer a sealed lost-property envelope, let Reed keep the book, or leave the decision for later. The note is not published or treated as a clue.

All three branches return to a four-option hub. The player may finish after any number of visitors. Each outcome has a distinct epilogue line, with no invented future purchases, author identifications, or agreements.

## Regression checklist

1. Every one of the nine branch choices reaches the hub, records its specific outcome, and hides its completed visitor.
2. All three visitors can be completed in a single playthrough, in any order.
3. Leaving the counter returns to the main reading, and the encounter does not reappear.
4. Epilogue statements match actual choices and do not claim delivery, permission or access.
5. The conversation does not grant manuscript evidence flags or access to Iris's papers.
