# Books and investigations — Episode 01 design draft

## Catalog design principles
Books are fictional; each has genre, synopsis, motifs, audience, sensitive themes, and a *reason* a particular character might like it. More than one recommendation can be plausible. Never use a recommendation as mind control or a hidden morality test.

## Order 1 — Miller
**Request:** 'A mystery where I can solve it before the detective, and nobody dies in chapter one.' **Viable:** B13 (strong puzzle, darker tone) or B04 (gentler family mystery). **Follow-up:** ask if she prefers clever structure or emotional stakes. **Payoff:** whichever book she picks changes a later conversation topic, not the objective facts of her rumor.

## Order 2 — Mara
**Request:** 'A gift for someone who keeps rereading the same final page.' **Viable:** B03 (future-oriented), B11 (shared railway interests), B06 (if she values difficult conversations). **Follow-up:** does he like nonfiction? Is the gift meant to comfort or challenge? **Payoff:** Leo later mentions the book; Mara feels heard if recommendation reflects what she actually described.

## Order 3 — Iris
**Request:** 'Something about coming back somewhere that doesn't quite feel like home.' **Viable:** B02, B08, B16. **Follow-up:** does she want a hopeful ending or a truthful one? **Payoff:** can unlock a conversation about her return without interrogating her about Leo.

## Order 4 — Anna
**Request:** 'A story where the characters learn to talk to each other, but not one that pretends a conversation fixes everything.' **Viable:** B07, B10, B14. **Follow-up:** wants fiction or essays? **Payoff:** may mention the difference between honesty and being pressured to confess.

## Investigation A — The reservation box (evening 1)
**Question:** Who was supposed to receive the blue novel? **Clues:** reservation card with Leo's name, dated receipt, book spine, unopened addressed envelope. **Solution:** identify Leo as recipient; **not** 'Leo read the letter.' **Mistake path:** if player misidentifies, notebook flags 'unverified' and offers review without punitive lockout. **Ethical choice:** preserve seal or open it. The exterior alone suffices to continue.

## Investigation B — Two versions of the goodbye (evening 2)
**Question:** Can both Iris's and Leo's accounts be sincerely believed? **Clues:** Iris says she left a message, Leo says he never received one, emergency closure date, boxed reservation. **Deduction:** the delivery failed. **Not deducible yet:** whether either still loves the other. **Hint ladder:** compare dates → look at card → recall shop closure.

## Investigation C — Wednesday rumor (day 2–3)
**Question:** Is a witnessed meeting proof of an affair? **Clues:** Miller's sighting, location near support center, Thomas's evasions, Nora's refusal to discuss clients. **Valid answer:** insufficient evidence; ask for consent-based clarification rather than spread an allegation. **Later canonical reveal:** Thomas discusses debt/support meetings if he chooses; player cannot extract private details from Nora. **Wrong action consequence:** rumor spreads, Anna may distance herself; no game-over.

## Pacing / usability
Each book has a short readable summary and an optional expanded excerpt. Player may ask up to 2–4 useful questions per major order; avoid 20-question interrogations. Night notebook links statements to their sources. Puzzle solutions must be reachable without opening sealed mail or violating a character's privacy.
