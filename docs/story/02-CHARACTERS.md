# Episode 01 — Character Bible (draft)
All characters are fictional. Each knows only what is explicitly described; never infer universal knowledge from a global quest flag.

## Iris Bell (28) — illustrator, returned home
**Public face:** sharp, observant, deflects personal questions with dry humor. **Need:** decide whether Bellwick can be a home again without reopening old wounds. **Fear:** being treated as a cautionary tale. **Private truth:** wrote the letter and assumed Leo read it. **Knows:** she gave book to Eleanor; never saw Leo at platform. **Does not know:** closure leak or missing delivery; Leo's actual feelings; what Mara knows. **Voice:** concise, image-driven metaphors, sarcasm when nervous. **Trust trigger:** asking what she wants now, not who she used to love. **Boundary:** she can refuse to discuss Leo.

## Leo Marsh (29) — local railway timetable clerk
**Public face:** reliable, practical, remembers everyone's travel times. **Need:** stop treating the past as a personal verdict. **Fear:** making commitments he cannot keep. **Private belief:** Iris deliberately left without explanation. **Knows:** their old friendship; his current relationship with Mara. **Does not know:** letter exists until told; Iris's reason for return. **Voice:** concrete and measured, occasional understated joke. **Boundary:** does not volunteer romantic history to a stranger.

## Mara Finch (28) — baker, Leo's partner
**Public face:** generous and direct, notices who actually listens. **Need:** be chosen honestly rather than protected with half-truths. **Fear:** becoming a footnote in someone else's reunion story. **Knows:** Iris and Leo knew each other; their current relationship has real value. **Does not know:** letter, its contents, or the circumstances of its loss. **Voice:** lively, food analogies, direct follow-up questions. **Agency:** may stay, leave, or request space; not an obstacle NPC.

## Eleanor Vale (64) — former bookshop owner
**Public face:** warm but imperfect, keeps meticulous notes except under pressure. **Need:** take responsibility for an accidental harm without controlling how others resolve it. **Knows:** emergency closure, boxed reservations, old intended delivery. **Does not know:** current romantic relationships. **Voice:** careful, gently humorous, apologizes without demanding forgiveness. **Boundary:** will not disclose contents of sealed correspondence she never read.

## Mrs. Beatrice Miller (71) — regular customer and rumor hub
**Public face:** exuberant, funny, impatient with silence. **Need:** feel connected to a changing town. **Fear:** becoming irrelevant. **Knows:** saw Thomas with Nora outside the support center. **Believes:** possible affair. **Does not know:** reason for meetings. **Voice:** affectionate diminutives, digressions, leading questions. **Growth:** can learn to correct a rumor if confronted with harm, not instant villain redemption.

## Anna Hale (32) — teacher
**Public face:** composed, practical, reads historical fiction. **Need:** rebuild honest communication with Thomas. **Fear:** looking foolish in public. **Knows:** Thomas has been distant, hiding money stress; not the specifics. **Does not know:** support group or Nora's professional role. **Voice:** complete sentences, guarded jokes. **Boundary:** may ask player not to spread what she shared.

## Thomas Hale (34) — electrician
**Public face:** hardworking, awkward with emotional vocabulary. **Need:** acknowledge debt and ask for help. **Fear:** disappointing Anna. **Knows:** counseling/support group and debt. **Does not know:** Miller is spreading a rumor unless player tells him. **Voice:** practical details, short evasions when ashamed. **Boundary:** does not need to prove innocence to a stranger.

## Nora Reed (42) — community counselor
**Public face:** reserved, compassionate, protective of clients. **Need:** keep the support center trusted. **Knows:** Thomas attends support group. **Cannot disclose:** private details about attendees without permission. **Voice:** clear, kind boundaries. **Narrative role:** her refusal to confirm confidential information is not evidence of guilt.

## Player character — bookseller (details TBD)
New to Bellwick and therefore plausibly unfamiliar with local relationships. Owns the shop and can recommend books, keep a notebook and ask questions; not a trained investigator. Player may opt out of personal questions and still serve customers. The game should not assume the player is female or name them after a real person without explicit customization.
